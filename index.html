Burn faster

Switches climate on through Tessie

Climate on full: AC at LO when it's warm out, HI plus seat and wheel heaters when it's cold. Max defrost runs heat and AC together, the most the cabin can use. Finish parked turns on Camp mode if you're home above the target.

Needs your Tessie token (Setup). Only climate commands are sent. At the target, on Stop, or after a day, the app switches off what it switched on and puts the cabin temperature back. It can only do that while this page is open.
